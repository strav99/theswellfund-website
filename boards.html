Board bio

For nominating committees

Scott Travasos is a finance and operations executive and former professional sports general manager based in Encinitas, California. He has served as chief financial officer or chief operating officer of a $150M statewide education foundation, Business for Social Responsibility and the Blue Shield of California Foundation, and is partner and COO of a family office focused on coastal San Diego real estate, one of three family offices he has worked with. He was president and general manager of the 2002 WUSA champion Carolina Courage, founded the international soccer nonprofit GreaterGoal, and served as the finance lead on the board of the Bay Area Sports Organizing Committee during the U.S. Olympic bid process. He co-founded Swell Property and Remain Life Insurance and is writing a book, FieldWork, on how lessons from the playing field translate to work. He holds an MBA from Wake Forest University.
