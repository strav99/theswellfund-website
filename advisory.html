What Scott does

Fractional CFO and family office operator

Scott steps in as the finance and operations lead for organizations that need a CFO's judgment without a CFO's headcount: family offices, foundations, membership organizations and founder-led companies.

  • Budgets, rolling forecasts, financial models and the board and committee reporting built on them.
  • Finance, audit and investment committee support, including investment policy and manager oversight.
  • Cash forecasting, treasury and banking relationships.
  • Enterprise finance systems: Workday, Salesforce, Sage Intacct, Blackbaud and Microsoft Dynamics, including selection, configuration and migration.
  • Legal, insurance and risk coordination across multiple entities, with outside counsel and brokers.
  • Real estate for family offices: acquisition, redevelopment from one home site to fifty or more, entitlement and engineering, and disposition.

The Swell Fund

Partner and Chief Operating Officer, family office

  • Principal operator coordinating advisors, counsel, brokers and development teams on complex, high-discretion assets.
  • Finance, risk management, legal and insurance coordination, and governance support across multiple entities.

Statewide education foundation

Chief Financial Officer

One of five executives on the leadership team of a 650-employee statewide foundation with $150M in annual operating expenses and $5B in annual fund flows, supporting 1.9 million community college students.

  • Directed a 43-member finance department and oversaw a 100-member back-office operation.
  • Financial planning across 600+ projects funded by state, federal, corporate and philanthropic sources, with project-level and consolidated budgets, forecasts and variance reporting.
  • Chaired the Finance and Investment Committees overseeing a $150M corpus in coordination with J.P. Morgan.
  • Directed Workday and Salesforce supporting 10,000+ annual financial transactions and 9,000+ expense reports.
