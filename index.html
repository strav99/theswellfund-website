Scott Travasos has spent more than twenty years running finance and operations for a championship professional soccer club, a $150M statewide education foundation, a global corporate sustainability network and three family offices. He has been the CFO who presents to the board and the president who signs the players.

His career is bracketed by the Olympic movement: his first paycheck came from the Atlanta Olympic Games, and years later he held the finance seat on the Bay Area's board for the U.S. Olympic bid. In between came the Carolina Courage and GreaterGoal, then Swell Property in Encinitas and most of the last decade as a CFO and developer of coastal San Diego real estate with an eye for design.

  • 2002WUSA champions, Carolina Courage, as President and GM
  • $5Bannual fund flows as CFO of a statewide education foundation
  • 12+countries, with GreaterGoal, the soccer nonprofit he founded, and BSR, whose offices ran from Paris to Beijing
  • MBAWake Forest University, where he founded the Sports Management Club
